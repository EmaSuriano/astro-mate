Mate is an Astro portfolio theme built for clarity: warm type, sparse chrome, and content you keep in a GitHub Gist.

Four sections ship ready:

- **Landing** — a typographic greeting with your name, roles, and socials.
- **About** — who you are, with an optional portrait.
- **Projects** — selected work as an editorial index.
- **Writing** — recent posts pulled from DEV.to.

Point the Gist at your profile JSON, adjust copy, and publish. The page should read like print—not a Tailwind template.
